CUNYfirst Registration Step-by-Step Guide

Source: https://enrollmentmanagement.baruch.cuny.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/18/2020/10/How-to-register.pdf

This document is a PDF. Key steps covered:
1. Log into CUNYfirst at home.cunyfirst.cuny.edu
2. Click Self Service > Student Center
3. Click 'Search for Classes' in the upper right
4. Select term, enter subject/course number, click Search
5. Select a section and click 'Next'
6. Review the class and click 'Finish Enrolling'
7. Check your enrollment status — green check = success
8. To add yourself to a waitlist, select 'Wait List' when a class is full
9. Monitor your Student Center for waitlist updates
10. Clear all holds before your registration date or you will be blocked
